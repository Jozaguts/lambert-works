# Research: Wood Deck Staining Near Plymouth Meeting, PA

## Search intent
Commercial-local service support for homeowners deciding whether to stain, repair, or refresh an exterior wood deck.

## Evidence used
- Benjamin Moore, "How to Stain a Deck": https://www.benjaminmoore.com/en-us/interior-exterior-paints-stains/how-to-advice/exteriors/stain-a-deck
- Search Console export: `docs/data/Consultas.csv`

## Content points
- Preparation is central: clean the surface, remove loose finish, allow the wood to dry, and assess weathered or soft areas.
- New or previously coated wood may need different preparation.
- Test color/opacity in an inconspicuous location and follow the product data sheet.
- Stain cannot correct structural movement, widespread rot, or unsafe framing.
- Cover local intent around Plymouth Meeting, Blue Bell, Skippack, Whitemarsh Township, Lafayette Hill, Conshohocken, and Montgomery County.
