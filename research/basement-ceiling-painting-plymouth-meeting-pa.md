## Content points
- Address leaks and moisture before finishing basement surfaces; paint is not a moisture-control solution.
- An exposed ceiling can keep utilities accessible while using color to visually organize the space.
- Preparation must protect floors, walls, equipment, lights, pipes, ducts, and stored items.
- Color choice depends on ceiling height, lighting, utility layout, and desired accessibility.
- Keep specialist boundaries clear for electrical, plumbing, structural, mold, and waterproofing work.
